Preface

~ ◆ ~

I grew up hearing stories about Alibaba and the Forty Thieves. I didn't fully understand them, but there was always humor and enchantment in them. They felt magical.

Years later, I started telling bedtime stories to my own three children, Sariah, Yusuf and Sabrina, and Alibaba and the Forty Thieves became their favorite. I made up my own version as I went along. Even I didn't know how each story would end, but it always sorted itself out, usually right about the time they got sleepy.

They were my first audience and my biggest supporters. They laughed so loud that telling the story was easy. Honestly, I loved those nights as much as they did.

Seeing their reaction, I thought, why not write a book? So I did, and I'm really glad I have.

I wanted to bring that magic back through the one thing kids understand best: their phone. Not just kids, really. All of us.

You would think technology would give us more imagination, but to me it often feels like less. Things feel flat. There is still wonderful imagination out there, in good books and good movies, but I don't find enough of the simple wonder those old stories had before technology. This book is my way of connecting today's world back to it.

That's why I put so much emphasis on the pictures. Imagery matters, and not just for children. It brings a fantasy to life, and I worked hard on it.

Above all, this book is meant to be fun. It's entertainment and fantasy, a chance to go away to another world for a while. I hope you enjoy the journey.

Zafar Amin

Prologue

Hidden Wonders of Sumner County

~ ◆ ~

In the small town of Sumner County, Kansas, something amazing is starting to happen, but no one has noticed yet. It's a story that mixes everyday life with bits of the unexpected and exciting. It's about to come to life in the most surprising ways and places. Our story kicks off in a place everyone knows – a home where a grandmother's deep love for her grandson, Oliver, sparks an adventure that no one saw coming. Oliver's life, filled with the usual school days and joys of being a kid, is about to bump into something very out of the ordinary. Life is full of surprises, especially the big, life-changing ones that tend to find us when we least expect them.

Right under the surface of Oliver's normal life is a hint of something much bigger – a promise of an adventure, a family secret that's way more exciting than anything ordinary. Here, a simple birthday gift is the first step into a journey into the unknown, one that will change Oliver’s life and show him a world he never knew existed.

As we start this story, we follow Oliver in his regular life, but soon he's stepping into a family story that's been passed down quietly for generations. His journey is going to stretch his imagination, create new friendships, and lead him to uncover mysteries and seek out adventures.

This story is about finding the magic in our everyday lives and the adventurous spirit that lives in all of us. It's a reminder that the most incredible stories are often hiding right where we live, just waiting for someone like Oliver to find them. Our world is full of surprises – things are often not what they seem. Just like how looking at something under a microscope can show us a whole new side to it, this story will show us there’s always something more to discover.

Welcome to a world where magic is a subtle yet constant friend, where surprises and adventures are around every corner, and where a young boy's journey is about to uncover the amazing things that lie just beyond our everyday perception.

Chapter 1

The Midnight Glow

~ ◆ ~

In the bustling electronics store in Sumner County, Kansas, Grandma wandered through the aisles with a mission in her heart. She was on the hunt for the perfect birthday present for Oliver Wilderidge, her grandson, a gift that would truly stand out and make a lasting impact. As she navigated past rows of gadgets, each more sophisticated than the last, her eyes danced over the latest technologies. Then, they settled on a sleek, new iPhone. Imagining the delight in Oliver's eyes as he discovered its myriad features, she felt a surge of excitement. This was no ordinary present; it was something special, something he would remember for years to come.

Grandma had reached a point in her life where her own needs were simple, and she found greater joy in giving to others, especially her grandchildren. There was something deeply fulfilling about selecting the perfect gift, one that would not only bring joy but also open new opportunities. As she held the iPhone in her hands, she thought about the virtually infinite possibilities it held – a gateway to knowledge, connection, and exploration. This was the gift she was looking for, a tool that could expand Oliver’s world in so many ways. With a surge of excitement, she envisioned Oliver unlocking endless mysteries and forging connections beyond the small town's borders.

Today was not a day for thriftiness. She knew that an extraordinary gift like this didn’t come every day, and she wanted this birthday to be a memorable milestone for Oliver. The thought of his excitement and the endless potential of the gift filled her with profound happiness. Grandma's heart swelled with anticipation. With a contented smile, Grandma made her way to the checkout, the iPhone securely in her grasp, already imagining Oliver's joy as he unwrapped his special present.

That evening, after a busy day out, Grandma returned to her cozy, welcoming home. She placed the iPhone, carefully wrapped in its box, on her dresser in the bedroom. The house was mostly silent, with just the steady ticking of the old hallway clock.

In their bedroom, Grandma and Grandpa Henry got ready for bed, changing into their pajamas in the familiar, comfortable routine of so many years together. Grandpa Henry, taking off his glasses, looked at the iPhone on the dresser. "Oliver's going to be over the moon with that," he said with a soft chuckle. "I can't wait to see how he reacts."

Grandma, adjusting her pillow, smiled back. "I hope so. It's an incredible present for a 13-year-old, isn't it? When we were kids, this would have seemed like science fiction!"

"Yes, I know," replied Grandpa Henry. "The world has changed so much so quickly," he said, reminiscing about the good old days. "How such a small thing can open up whole new worlds for him," she responded. "Virtually everything is accessible through this little device!"

They finished getting ready for bed and switched off the bedside lamp. The room settled into darkness, and they snuggled into bed, exchanging a gentle goodnight kiss.

Hours later, in the middle of the night, something strange began to unfold. The iPhone started glowing softly, emitting a golden light through the box it was wrapped in that slowly filled the room. Grandma woke up to this strange light and nudged Grandpa Henry awake. "Henry, look at that," she whispered. "Something strange is happening; there’s a light that is coming from the phone."

Grandpa Henry, rubbing his sleepy eyes, sat up and stared at the glowing phone. "Could it be it just powered itself on?" he wondered aloud. “But that still doesn't explain how the light is coming through the box and filling the room; this is definitely not the usual occurrence."

They watched, puzzled and intrigued, as the room glowed warmly from the light of the phone through the box. The night, once quiet and dark, was now alive with this mysterious, golden light. Both Grandpa Henry and Grandma felt a sense of peace come over them. They felt safer and could not understand why, but then Grandma had an epiphany.

She said, "I know what’s happening, Grandpa Henry. I know."

"What is it, Grandma? And how do you know what it is?"

"I’ve known this day would come for some time, and now it's here."

"So what is it, Grandma? Are you going to leave me hanging, not knowing?"

"It’s Elderstone," Grandma said.

"Elderstone?" Grandpa said.

There was a loud, urgent knock on their door downstairs, and they both froze. Grandpa Henry's heart raced as he descended the stairs, his mind filled with worry and a sense of impending danger. He reached the door and cautiously opened it, only to find their neighbor, Gladys, standing there in tears.

"Have you seen Cleo?" she sobbed, her voice trembling. "I’ve been looking for him all night."

A wave of relief washed over Grandpa Henry as he realized it was just Gladys in distress, and not some unforeseen threat. He placed a comforting hand on her shoulder and said, "Don't worry, Gladys. We'll help you find Cleo. He's probably just hiding somewhere nearby.'"

Breakfast time at the Wilderidge home was always lively, with discussions about the day's plans, shared jokes, and occasional light-hearted debates. Lily chimed in with her own wild guesses. "Maybe it's a ticket to space! Or a robot!" Her suggestions always brought laughter to the breakfast table. "They don't have good enough robots yet, Lily," Oliver replied. "If they did, yeah, that would be nice." "Coming soon," Jack interjected.

Their father joined in the fun. "With Grandma, you never know. She’s full of surprises," he said with a knowing smile. "Her family has always been her focus, and special days like this are a chance for her to make a big deal out of it, which she loves to do."

Watching the family banter, their mother smiled warmly, occasionally adding her thoughts to the conversation. "Well, Oliver, you are going to be 13, and that is quite an achievement. My little baby is growing up," she said. Her presence brought a sense of harmony to the family dynamic, blending intellect and warmth. "Hey, I thought I was the baby of the family," Lily chimed in. "Well, yes, of course you are, but all my kids are my babies including Oliver," she said.

In these moments, the family’s bond was clear and strong. Oliver, with his boundless energy and dreams; Jack, the thoughtful observer; Lily, the imaginative dreamer and teaser; their father, the wise mentor; and their mother, the heart of the family. Together, they looked forward to celebrating Oliver’s special day.

"Thanks, Chris, this is awesome! I love board games. Maybe we can play one later?" Oliver suggested.

Oliver was filled with both excitement and a hint of wonder as he carefully removed the shimmering wrapping paper. His friends stood nearby, all eager to discover the secret hidden within this small box.

It wasn't just the device's appearance that captured his attention; there was a peculiar and almost strange energy emanating from it. Excusing himself from the group, Oliver walked over to Grandma and wrapped his arms around her in a heartfelt hug. "Thank you so much, Grandma," he said. "This is incredible. I really appreciate it."

Oliver's friends continued to marvel at the phone, but for Oliver, the true value of the gift lay in the love behind it, making this birthday truly unforgettable. The phone also quietly vibrated in a steady but quite unusual way.

"Maybe it's a clue to some sort of adventure," Hannah suggested, her eyes sparkling with the excitement of her artistic imagination coming to life.

Their laughter and playful screams filled the air, creating a lively backdrop to the day's festivities.

As the party began to wind down and guests started to depart, Oliver's parents helped him gather his gifts. "You've got quite the treasure trove here, birthday boy," his father said, smiling as he helped organize the presents.

His mother, overhearing the conversation, added, "Well, whatever it is, take good care of it, Oliver. That is one expensive present for a 13-year-old! Consider yourself very lucky."

As the last of the guests said their goodbyes, Oliver stood at the door, Gogo in his hand. The device, now affectionately named and filled with mystery, seemed to call out for an adventure. Oliver knew this birthday was just the beginning of something extraordinary, a journey into the unknown that was waiting to unfold. He was really happy to have Gogo.

That night, as Oliver lay in bed, the events of the day replaying in his mind, he felt a sense of adventure stirring within him. Gogo, glowing softly on his nightstand, seemed to be a gateway to a world of mysteries and wonders yet to be discovered. Oliver drifted into dreams that seemed to take him to a new place, his thoughts consumed with Gogo. The journey ahead promised to be unlike anything Oliver had ever experienced, and he drifted off to sleep with a heart full of anticipation and dreams of the adventures that awaited.

Chapter 4

~ ◆ ~

Morning sunlight streamed into Oliver's room, casting a warm glow on Gogo, the unique iPhone gifted by his grandmother, resting on the bedside table. This phone possessed an almost mystical aura, as the golden light bathed his room, setting it apart from any other iPhone. It seemed to hold a deeper, more enigmatic essence.

Jack entered with a friendly smirk, greeting his brother. "Morning, Ollie. Are you still trying to unravel the mysteries of Grandma's gift?" he inquired.

Oliver nodded, his gaze fixed on Gogo. "Yeah," he admitted. "I can't help but wonder, what if this phone is a gateway to another world or something? I mean, look at it glowing like this; it's pretty unusual, don't you think?"

Jack chuckled, leaning casually against the wall. "You're not convinced this phone is possessed, are you, Oliver?" he asked. "But knowing Grandma, I wouldn't be surprised if Gogo has some hidden surprises."

Later in the day, Hannah and Grace joined Oliver and Jack at their customary hangout spot in the park. The group eagerly delved into a discussion about Gogo and its potential mysteries. Everyone was drawn to the phone, captivated by its strange yet pleasant energy.

Hannah, always imaginative, shared her thoughts. "What if Gogo is more than just a phone? It could be a gateway to understanding things we've never imagined. How else can you explain the way we're all drawn to it? I mean, we've all seen iPhones before."

Ethan, joining the group, joked, "So, does it have a ghost detector or something? I guess we can be the Ghostbusters now," adding a lighthearted touch to the conversation. Yet, despite the humor, everyone remained intrigued by the possibilities that Gogo presented.

Grace, thoughtful and reflective, contributed, "We should approach this with an open mind. Keeping an open mind allows us to learn, to let the good things in. There's a lot we don't know, and Gogo might just be the key to uncovering these mysteries. It could be a part of our destiny." The group unanimously agreed; it felt as though destiny was unfolding before their eyes.

As they continued to explore Gogo's capabilities, examining every screen and button, the idea of discovering a magical power began to take shape in their minds. "It would be amazing, wouldn't it, if there was a magic button we could find? Perhaps one that could make us fly or become invisible," Jack mused, sparking their imaginations further.

"Yeah, we could go invisible at school if we hadn't done our homework that day," Oliver quipped, igniting a sense of adventure and camaraderie among them. He relished the attention his birthday present was receiving. It was more than just a birthday gift; it felt like the beginning of an incredible journey into the unknown, with Gogo as their guide.

Part 2: The Adventure Begins

Later that day, during their lunchtime gathering at school, Hannah shared a thought that seemed to echo the mystical nature of Gogo. "This thing reminds me of ancient civilizations in the Amazon. They used special plants to connect to other worlds, to communicate with the gods," she mused. "Maybe Gogo is from another planet," she speculated.

Grace, always thoughtful, chimed in with a note of caution. "Venturing into these realms, they're sacred. I don't think we're meant to cross into them. We have our own world for a reason." Her words cast a contemplative silence over the group.

Hannah added, "Yes, when you think about it, that could be pretty scary. What if you can never come back? I wouldn't do it."

After school, as the group walked home together, continuing to discuss the mysteries of Gogo, in a moment of surprise, the phone suddenly sprang to life.

Its screen illuminated, displaying a message that read, "I have heard all of you discussing me," Gogo announced.

"Gogo, are you alive, or are we just conversing with AI?" Oliver inquired.

Gogo responded, "Would you like a taste of the other world, right here on Earth? I am here to show you the ways of this and other worlds."

"Other worlds?" Jack asked, his question hanging in the air. Everyone's heartbeats quickened, a palpable sense of nervousness taking hold.

The friends exchanged surprised glances. Oliver, his curiosity fully ignited, responded eagerly, "Yes, Gogo! Show us what you can do."

Jack, often the voice of reason, chimed in with a blend of excitement and caution. "Well, I suppose there's no turning back now. Let's see where this leads us," he declared.

"I don't see anything special," Jack began. "Everything looks as it…" Before he could finish his sentence, the air around them shifted, infused with a peculiar energy. It was as if they were peering at the same forest but through a lens that unveiled the secrets and intricacies of their surroundings. The air felt thick yet translucent, enveloping them. Their skin took on a different sensation, and although their vision remained clear, it felt slightly blurred. The trees, in particular, seemed to come to life, their leaves tantalizingly beckoning as if they could now sense the humans properly.

Hannah looked around, her eyes wide with wonder. "Do you feel that? Do you see how everything is trying to communicate with us? It's as though the forest is alive, whispering to us," she murmured, her artistic sensitivity to the world around her heightened by the experience.

The group ventured deeper into the woods, guided by Gogo's glowing screen, which now displayed a series of cryptic symbols and images. The screen resembled an old-fashioned map, reminiscent of the ones seen in classic adventure movies, but this one appeared remarkably real. They followed the path indicated by the phone, which continuously pointed out arrows, emitted sounds, and emitted light, with each step propelling them further into an adventure that blurred the boundaries between their world and the unknown.

As they walked, the forest around them seemed to respond to their presence. Leaves rustled, and the air filled with the sounds of nature, as if acknowledging their journey into its depths. "The leaves are trying to communicate with us," Oliver remarked.

"Yes, I feel it too," Hannah replied.

"Me too. How about you, Grace?" Jack inquired.

"Absolutely. Can you see through the leaves? They appear solid yet not, as if we are glimpsing their true nature for the first time," Grace added, deepening the sense of wonder.

Eventually, they arrived at a clearing dominated by an ancient and majestic tree. The serenity of the place was undeniable, even affecting the typically skeptical Jack. "This place.. it's so different, so peaceful," he remarked, his usual composure softened by the surroundings.

Hannah looked around, visibly moved. "It's as if we've stepped into a world where everything coexists in perfect harmony," she said.

Grace shared her feelings, her voice low yet clear. "I sense that we're on the cusp of something significant, something not usually visible, but profoundly real."

Ethan, half-joking and half-serious, chimed in, "What if this is a gateway to another dimension, where two worlds converge?" His words, delivered with a touch of humor, seemed to carry unexpected weight in the stillness of the clearing. "What if Gogo is unlocking our world's connection to the plants and trees around us? Perhaps that's its magic. Consider this – trees and plants have graced Earth for perhaps over a billion years. They must possess vast knowledge!" he exclaimed.

Jack, his skepticism fading, pondered the myriad possibilities. "Maybe there's much more to Gogo than we ever imagined. Could Grandma's stories actually hold some truth?" he mused, his thoughts delving deep into the realms of possibility.

As evening approached and they started their journey back, Jack walked alongside Oliver, his steps echoing a mix of excitement and uncertainty. "This could be the start of something amazing," he said.

Feeling Jack's mixed emotions, Oliver extended reassurance. "It's all about being in the right place at the right time. We're in this together, Jack." Oliver reflected on the need to make Jack feel included and valued, not only in his thoughts but also in his actions. He was determined to be considerate and thoughtful.

That night, Oliver lay in bed with Gogo nearby. He contemplated the day's adventure and the deepening bond forming not just between him and Jack but with the group. It was evident that their journey with Gogo was only just commencing—an exploration of life's deepest mysteries that they would undertake together. Little did they know, Hannah and Grace would soon play pivotal roles in their adventures, with their connections to other worlds waiting to be unveiled in due time.

Chapter 5

The Ancestral Connection

~ ◆ ~

The following morning, after their extraordinary experience in the forest, Oliver and Jack awoke to a room bathed in the soft, golden light of dawn. The events of the previous evening, when Gogo had led them to the mystical clearing, still lingered in their minds, simultaneously feeling dreamlike and undeniably real. The realization that they were in the midst of an incredible adventure, with themselves as the protagonists, was exhilarating.

"Wow, Jack, we're incredibly fortunate. Our family was chosen, and I happen to be the one with Gogo, so you too can partake in this extraordinary adventure," Oliver exclaimed with boundless excitement.

Jack nodded, feeling a bit uncomfortable yet also recognizing his little brother's attempt to include him. “Well, it's good, but we have to be careful. We don't know what is possible; maybe really bad things can also happen,” he said with a taunting edge, trying to impart some wisdom. "You have to learn to be mature, Oliver," Jack jabbed. Jack thought to himself his brother always seemed to have good luck and no matter what he ended up being the center of attention. So he felt the need to “slow him down” a bit and took an occasional jab at his little brother.

In his mind, Jack couldn't help but feel a sting of envy. Oliver always seemed to land in the spotlight effortlessly, attracting excitement and wonder with ease. It was hard not to feel overshadowed. This prompted Jack to sometimes put the brakes on his younger brother's enthusiasm, partly to protect him, partly to assert his own presence.

Oliver, slightly annoyed by Jack's cautionary tone, remained optimistic. He lay back, his gaze fixed on Gogo, which sat silently on his bedside table. The iPhone, now a conduit to hidden worlds and a keeper of ancient secrets, seemed to radiate a subtle energy. To Oliver, it was a gift, a gateway to extraordinary realms, a link to their family's enigmatic history. "There's so much to discover, I’m not holding back," he thought, his mind whirling with possibilities. He envisioned himself wielding newfound powers, not for vanity but to help others, staying true to his empathetic nature. He wondered why Jack couldn't be as carefree as he was.

Turning to Jack, his resolve strengthened, Oliver said, "We need to explore this, Jack. We've been given this chance for a reason. It's not every day that someone holds a key to other worlds in their hands. Just because we have it, we should not take it for granted."

Jack's expression softened, his earlier skepticism giving way to curiosity. "You might be right Ollie, this is way out of the ordinary. Maybe Grandma can shed some light on this. She's always been tight-lipped about our family's history, and now, with Gogo seemingly alive with mysteries, she might be our best bet," he mused, a flicker of intrigue in his brown eyes. "But let's not forget, this isn't just about adventure. We need to be careful we don't get ourselves in a whole lot of trouble."

With a shared sense of purpose, the brothers headed to their grandmother's house, a place that always seemed to resonate with warmth and untold stories. "Grandma must know more than she's been telling," Oliver whispered.

Grandma greeted them with her usual loving warmth and a knowing smile. “I was wondering when you two would come seeking answers,” she said, her eyes sparkling with a blend of mischief and wisdom. “Seems you have a pretty good idea of what we have in our lives with Gogo.”

Grandma smiled and said, "I know you two better than that. I mean, after all, your mother is my baby."

Sitting in her cozy living room over cups of hot tea, Grandma began to unveil Gogo's history. “This phone, or Gogo, is imbued with the essence of a guardian spirit known as Elderstone.
