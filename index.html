The author

Zafar Amin

Zafar Amin is a builder, a songwriter and a storyteller. He founded IDDAS Technologies and its CandleMe platform, wrote and recorded the album Born Free, and sang and played guitar with the Los Angeles band The SandGuineas. Gogo Guardians began as bedtime stories for his three children, Sariah, Yusuf and Sabrina, his first audience.
