Privacy for Families

Children play. Parents buy. We never ask a child for anything.

What we collect from children

Nothing. Children never create an account, and the game and the online book never ask for a name, email, photo or any other personal information. There are no ads, no chat and no tracking inside the game or the book, and they do not load anything from other companies.

What stays on your device

Game progress, emeralds, music settings and the unlock code are saved only in your own browser on your own device. We cannot see them. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Speaking the answers

The "Say it" button uses your device's built-in voice typing (Apple on iPhone and iPad, Google on Android and Chrome). We never receive or store any recording. If you prefer, simply type the answers instead.

When a parent buys

  • Purchases are made by a parent or guardian through our store, Payhip, which handles payment and the receipt email. Payhip's own privacy policy covers that information.
  • We use the parent's email only to deliver the purchase and to answer questions. We never sell it.
  • Our website's front page and buy page may use standard advertising measurement for adults. These tools are never used inside the game or the book.

Questions or requests

Write to us any time through the Gogo Guardians store contact page, and we will answer.

This policy follows the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Last updated November 13, 2026.
